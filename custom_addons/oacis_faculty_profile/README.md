Oacis Faculty & Staff Profiles
=================================

Manages faculty member and administrative staff profiles for Oacis ERP.

Features:
- Faculty Member (Academic Staff) profiles with qualifications, publications, and workload
- Administrative Staff profiles with role assignments
- Auto-generated employee ID numbers
- Automatic partner/user account creation
- Employment lifecycle management (draft, active, on_leave, sabbatical, suspended, terminated, retired)
- Company-level isolation for multi-institution setups

Dependencies:
- oacis_base (security groups, mixin)
- oacis_security
- oacis_campus
- oacis_academic
- oacis_calendar
- oacis_student
